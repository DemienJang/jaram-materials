# 타이틀 이미지 방향

## 핵심 방향

책 한 권을 요약하는 일에서 시작해, 여러 정보와 생각을 자기 방식으로 다시 연결하는 창조적 편집으로 나아가는 장면을 표현한다. 책이나 AI를 직접 설명하는 이미지가 아니라, 펼쳐진 책과 흩어진 메모가 새로운 구조로 이어지는 조용한 순간을 담는다.

## 이미지 프롬프트

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: Korean blog title image, square 1:1
Primary request: Create a quiet editorial still life about moving from summarizing a book to creating a new idea through editing and recombination.
Scene/backdrop: A warm, slightly worn wooden desk in a calm study at early morning. One open book sits near the center, surrounded by loose paper notes, small index cards, pencil marks, and faint connecting lines. The scattered notes gradually form a clear, elegant constellation toward the open book, suggesting analysis becoming synthesis.
Subject: An open book, handwritten-looking but intentionally unreadable notes, index cards, pencil, and subtle thread-like connections between ideas.
Style/medium: Cinematic editorial still-life photography with restrained artistic realism, tactile paper texture, thoughtful literary mood, no people.
Composition/framing: Square composition with the main book and emerging network of notes centered and readable; keep important objects within the central safe area so the image remains meaningful when cropped to a very wide 4:1 banner.
Lighting/mood: Soft morning window light, gentle shadows, contemplative, curious, quietly hopeful rather than dramatic.
Color palette: Warm ivory paper, muted charcoal, walnut brown, subdued blue-gray accents, a small restrained amber highlight.
Materials/textures: Paper fibers, pencil graphite, worn book edges, matte wood, natural soft shadow.
Text (verbatim): None.
Constraints: No visible words, no legible letters, no logos, no watermark, no computer screen, no futuristic glowing AI interface, no overly literal infographic symbols.
Avoid: Generic stock-photo study desk, clutter without visual meaning, exaggerated technology imagery, bright saturated colors, text embedded in the image, central object placed too close to the edges.
